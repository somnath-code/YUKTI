# Code of conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Discuss the evidence and code. Welcome questions and criticism, give people room to respond, and respect privacy and attribution. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and sharing private information without permission are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the private repository’s maintainers. Include relevant context and remove personal information that the owner does not need. The owner will handle reports with discretion and may remove material or restrict participation where necessary.
